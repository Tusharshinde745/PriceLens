PriceLens

Good prices, found for you.

Pick something you've been eyeing. PriceLens keeps an eye on Amazon.in, Flipkart, Croma and Reliance Digital, shows you a full year of price history, and tells you the moment it falls to your number.

PriceLens is a demo project. Products marked live price come from store APIs and are refreshed when the sync runs. The rest are simulated sample data.
